## Grab financial raw data–in seconds, with one click

FinGrab is a Chrome extension that downloads historical prices (OHLCV) from Yahoo Finance as CSV files, for investors, researchers and traders who work in Excel, Google Sheets or their own analysis tools. Analyze financial data on your terms.

[Add FinGrab.app to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/fingrab%E2%80%93yahoo-finance-exp/blajbhgoiomncfkpcfgiibcicifklgpm)

5 free exports. No signup. Setup in under 2 minutes.

The FinGrab button on Yahoo Finance 

## How it works

Install the extension

Add FinGrab from the Chrome Web Store. Takes less than a minute.

Open Yahoo Finance

Navigate to any stock, ETF, or index on finance.yahoo.com.

Export as CSV

Click the FinGrab icon to download raw data instantly.

## What you can export

 Historical price data

Open, High, Low, Close, Volume (OHLCV) for any time range.

 Adjusted close

Prices adjusted for splits and dividends, so long-range charts stay consistent.

 Multiple time ranges

Daily, weekly, or monthly data across custom date ranges.

## Frequently asked questions

Is FinGrab free? 

Yes. 6 months of prices are free, always, in every interval. 1 Year, 2 Years, 5 Years and full history: 5 free exports, then the paid plan. No signup required.

What data formats are supported? 

CSV files compatible with Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice, Numbers, and any analysis tool that reads CSV.

Does it work with all Yahoo Finance pages? 

It works on quote pages: individual stock, ETF, and index pages on finance.yahoo.com.

Is my data stored anywhere? 

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to external servers.
